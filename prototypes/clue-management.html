Clue Board: where clues live

Three proposals, one prototype. In all three a clue is a Clue item of the module's own in the Items directory, never in anyone's inventory; collecting it puts it on the party's record and Mysteries shows it at once. Pick a proposal and a seat, then drag a Clue from the sidebar, click cards, open the sheets.

What you are looking at

AManage in Mysteries. The DM collects a clue by dragging it from the Items directory onto the Party sheet, onto a member's portrait, or onto the Mysteries window; or by Collect on the clue's sheet or in the GM strip at the top of Mysteries. Remove is the ✕ on a card, DM only.

  • Players see Mysteries only: the cards, their own read marks, the Reader.
  • Nothing on the Party sheet changes.

BA Clues tab on the Party sheet. The sheet gains a fifth tab. For the DM it is the management page: the collected list, a drop target, Remove. Players see the same list read-only, a second door into the record.

  • Mysteries has no GM strip; Collect lives on the clue's sheet and the tab.
  • Two places show one list.

CBoth. A's drops and strip, plus the Clues tab on the Party sheet for the DM only. Players never see the tab.

In every proposal

  • A Clue is an item type of its own in the Items directory, with a plain sheet: name, image, text, found at. Never in an inventory, never on a character.
  • Collected means on the party's record. Mysteries fills itself. The item stays as the DM's master; an edit updates the party's copy with a revision mark.
  • Read is marked when a player opens the card in Mysteries. The DM's opening marks nothing.
  • Where and when is stamped at collection: the scene the table is viewing, the calendar date when the world has one.
  • Gone: the Read column, the Clue loot type, the bookmark, held-by, the Party Stash hand-off logic.
