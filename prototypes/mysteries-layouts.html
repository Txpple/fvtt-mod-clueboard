Mysteries: three layouts

The same party record (the 21 clues on prod and three sample mysteries) in three shapes. In each, drag a card onto a mystery to pin it; click a mystery to open it. Which one lets you see the clues and the mysteries at the same time with the least fuss?

Seat
Mysteries ✕

What you are looking at

AA rail of mysteries. All Clues and Unassigned at the top of a left column, each mystery a row with its dot, count and solved mark, New mystery at the foot. The cards fill the right. Click a row to open it; the mystery's question and theory sit above its cards. Drag a card onto any row to pin it.

  • Both halves always in view; no tabs, no band.
  • The full cards stay as they are.
  • The least change from today.

BTwo panes. Every clue on the left, the chosen mystery on the right with its head and its pinned cards. Drag left to right to pin. The right pane's picker chooses which mystery is open.

  • Best for working one mystery hard.
  • Two grids of full cards: wants a wide window.

CLanes and a tray. Each mystery is a lane holding its clues as small tiles; the unassigned tray sits at the bottom. Drag a tile between lanes. Click a tile to open the Reader.

  • The whole case on one screen.
  • The cards shrink to a name and a kind: the text, readers and notes live in the Reader only.

In all three

  • A clue can sit in several mysteries, and wears a dot for each.
  • Unread cards for this seat glow gold at the edge.
  • Nothing here is built; the data is prod's as of today.
